Football is more than just a game. It's the sound of the ball hitting the back of the net. It's the roar of the crowd when their team scores. It's the mud on your boots and the sweat on your forehead. For ninety minutes, nothing else matters. Twenty-two players, one ball, and a world of passion. It’s a simple game, but it can make you feel the highest joy and the deepest heartbreak. That’s the beauty of football.
